ThatCatThor Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 19, 2026

ThatCatThor is a creative project focused on software, digital art, games, stories, educational content, and other creative projects.

We respect your privacy and believe you should understand when information may be collected while interacting with ThatCatThor.

Information You Provide
ThatCatThor may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

Your name or username
Your email address or other contact information
Messages, questions, feedback, or other communications
Information you choose to provide when discussing a custom project
We do not ask you to provide personal information unless it is reasonably necessary for the interaction or service you requested.

Purchases and Third-Party Services
ThatCatThor may link to or use third-party services such as Ko-fi, Gumroad, Linktree, Discord, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Twitch, and other online platforms.

When you visit, purchase from, communicate through, or otherwise use one of these services, information may be collected and processed by that service according to its own privacy policy and terms.

ThatCatThor does not control the independent privacy practices of those third-party services.

Payments
When products, downloads, memberships, donations, or other purchases are offered through third-party platforms, payment processing may be handled by those platforms or their payment providers.

Please review the privacy and payment policies of the service processing your transaction for information about how your payment information is handled.

How Information May Be Used
Information provided directly to ThatCatThor may be used to:

Respond to messages or questions
Provide requested products, services, or project communications
Address support issues
Improve our projects and content
Maintain necessary business or transaction records
We aim to retain only information reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes.

Analytics, Cookies, and Tracking
Third-party platforms linked to or used by ThatCatThor may use cookies, analytics, or other technologies. Their collection and use of that information is governed by their respective policies.

If ThatCatThor later operates services that directly use additional analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking technologies, this policy may be updated to explain those practices.

Children's Privacy
ThatCatThor creates some content that may be appropriate for families, but our services are not intended to solicit personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services and purchases as appropriate.

Sharing of Information
ThatCatThor does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to provide a service you requested, work with service providers, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect rights or security, or address fraud or misuse.

Your Information
If you have provided personal information directly to ThatCatThor and would like to ask about, correct, or request deletion of that information, you may contact us using the privacy contact method published by ThatCatThor.

Requests involving information held independently by a third-party platform should be directed to that platform.

Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as ThatCatThor grows, adds services, or changes how information is handled.

Material changes will be reflected in the published policy, along with an updated effective date.
